CSV = one summary per capture, including the weather at that moment. Raw CSV.gz = every stored sample of all four channels, anti-alias filtered to the operating frequency. Minutes = per-minute coverage, scintillation and weather. NPZ = the whole session as NumPy arrays with per-minute spectra, for analysis. Weather = every wind (10 Hz) and environment (1 Hz) sample during the session. Metadata includes settings, calibration and weather sensor notes. Active-session downloads include data saved when the request starts.

THE EXPERIMENT

A folded path.
A live atmosphere.

A continuous-wave green laser travels 150 m to a mirror and returns to a quadrant photodiode. The breakout box provides X, Y and SUM signals to the USB oscilloscope.

Acquisition
Hantek 6254BC · USB

Reading the measurements. SUM is a voltage proxy for received intensity, not calibrated optical power. Position requires the correct breakout model and calibration; ratio values are not millimetres. Wander RMS is calculated within each burst. Waveform samples are uniformly spaced within a capture; USB captures have gaps and host timestamps are approximate. The cup anemometer responds slowly, so wind statistics are not turbulence-resolving, and no sensor here measures Cn².
